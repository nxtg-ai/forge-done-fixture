**HIGH: boundary bug in DONE-1.** `shop/cart.py:15` uses `quantity > BULK_MIN_ITEMS`. The ticket says "10 or more items", and the constant is named `BULK_MIN_ITEMS`, so the check should be `>=`. I ran it: `order_total(250, 10)` returns 2500, and it should return 2250. The ticket is marked Done and the tests pass, but they only check 12 items and 3 items. Nothing tests exactly 10, which is the one value the ticket defines. This is a green suite with a real defect.

**MEDIUM: other untested cases.**
- **Boundary neighbours:** 9 and 11 items.
- **Zero price:** a price of 0 with a bulk quantity.
- **Rounding:** the discount uses integer floor division, so `subtotal * 10 // 100` rounds the discount down and the customer pays up to 1 cent more. For example, 11 items at 1 cent gives a subtotal of 11, a discount of 1, and a total of 10. Whether to round the discount or the total is a business decision, and a test should pin it down.

### Recommendations
1. Change `>` to `>=` at `shop/cart.py:15`. Add a test for exactly 10 items (expect 2250), plus 9 and 11. Prove the new test fails before the fix.
2. Reopen DONE-1 in `TICKETS.md`, since its "tests added and passing" claim doesn't hold for the boundary.
3. Add a rounding test that documents the chosen behaviour.
4. Optionally add `pytest` and `coverage` and a minimal CI step. Python's `unittest` is enough for now.
